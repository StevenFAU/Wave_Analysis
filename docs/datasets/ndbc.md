# NOAA National Data Buoy Center (NDBC)

> Datasheet following the project template (`docs/datasets/README.md`), after
> *Datasheets for Datasets* (Gebru et al. 2021). Registry id: `ndbc`.
> Adapter: `wave_analysis.sources.ndbc`. Status: **implemented (v0.1.0)**.

## Provider

NOAA National Weather Service, National Data Buoy Center, Stennis Space Center,
MS. NDBC operates its own buoys and C-MAN stations and redistributes data from
partner networks (IOOS regional associations, CDIP, international arrays). The
owner of each station is given in `activestations.xml` (`owner`, `pgm`).

## Purpose of dataset

Operational marine observations for forecasting and warnings. For this project
it provides (1) **physical wave references**: bulk parameters and full
directional spectra; (2) **context covariates**: wind, pressure, temperature,
currents; (3) **camera imagery** at a subset of buoys.

## Geographic coverage

U.S. coastal and offshore waters, the Great Lakes, and partner arrays
worldwide. The 2026-09-26 inventory lists **1,797** station identifiers across
all products (1,350 with historical standard-meteorological files).

## Temporal coverage

Varies by station. The oldest records begin in the 1970s. The historical
directional-spectrum archive generally starts in **1996**. Realtime files cover
the last **45 days**.

## Platforms

Moored buoys (several hull types: 3-m discus, 2.1-m foam, 6-m NOMAD, etc.),
C-MAN fixed stations, partner platforms, drifting buoys. Hull and payload are
given in `station_table.txt`, but **only for the current configuration**. Do
not assume past years used the same hull or payload (`schemas/station.py`).

## Instruments

Wave measurements come from hull motion (accelerometers or motion packages)
processed onboard into spectra. Directional stations derive the directional
Fourier coefficients from pitch-roll-heave or equivalent measurements.
Processing is described in NDBC Technical Document 03-01 (Earle 2003), which
supersedes TD 96-01 (Earle 1996); NDBC says the 1996 edition should no longer
be used. Key points (read in full, `docs/literature/source_verification.md`):

- **Sensors** by payload: fixed accelerometers, Datawell HIPPY 40 (heave,
  pitch, roll), 3-axis magnetometers, angular-rate and tilt sensors.
- **Records** of 20 min (40 min on one system), sampled at 1.5–2.56 Hz;
  spectra cut off at 0.35–0.50 Hz depending on the system.
- **Spectra are computed on the buoy.** Raw heave, pitch and roll series are
  not transmitted (satellite messages are too short); further processing and
  QC happen on shore. So no published NDBC product gives the hull's attitude
  at the moment a camera image was taken (see *Open research questions*).
- **Uncertainty of one record:** 90 % confidence intervals for H_m0 are about
  −10 % to +15 %. This is the noise floor of a single-record label.
- Payloads deployed after 2003 are not covered; check conventions against
  data, as done for WVHT below.

| File (realtime / historical dir, code) | Content | Parsed as |
|---|---|---|
| `.txt` / `stdmet`, `h` | WDIR WSPD GST WVHT DPD APD MWD PRES ATMP WTMP DEWP VIS PTDY TIDE | `parse_stdmet` |
| `.spec` / — | WVHT SwH SwP WWH WWP SwD WWD STEEPNESS APD MWD | `parse_spectral_summary` |
| `.data_spec` / `swden`, `w` | S(f) [m²/Hz] (+ separation frequency in realtime) | `parse_spectral` |
| `.swdir` / `swdir`, `d` | α₁(f) mean direction [deg] | `parse_spectral` |
| `.swdir2` / `swdir2`, `i` | α₂(f) principal direction [deg] | `parse_spectral` |
| `.swr1` / `swr1`, `j` | r₁(f) [–] | `parse_spectral` (÷100 historical) |
| `.swr2` / `swr2`, `k` | r₂(f) [–] | `parse_spectral` (÷100 historical) |
| `.adcp` / `adcp`, `a` | current profile DEPnn DIRnn SPDnn | `parse_adcp` |
| — / `adcp2`, `b` | extended ADCP diagnostics | raw only (v0.1) |
| `.ocean` / `ocean`, `o` | T, S, O₂, turbidity, … | `parse_stdmet` (generic) |
| `buoycams.php` | latest camera image per station | `buoycam.snapshot_cameras` |

The directional spreading function is (NDBC measdes)
D(f,θ) = π⁻¹[½ + r₁cos(θ−α₁) + r₂cos 2(θ−α₂)], with directions in
nautical "coming-from" degrees. The project also offers the maximum-entropy
estimate from the same coefficients (`processing.spectra.directional_distribution`).

## Sampling frequency

Bulk meteorology is usually hourly, and every 10 minutes at many modern
stations. Waves are hourly or half-hourly depending on the payload. Wave records
are **20-minute acquisitions** in most current systems (WVHT is defined over
"the 20-minute sampling period"); 40-minute systems also exist (NDBC
acq.shtml).

## Timestamp convention

- All files use **UTC**.
- Meteorological data use an 8-min (buoy) or 2-min (C-MAN) acquisition, and
  since July 2004 the timestamp marks the **end** of acquisition.
- **Wave timestamps are rounded**: "to the nearest hour for 40 minute
  acquisition systems or nearest half hour for 20 minute acquisition systems."
  Payloads differ: DACT/VEEP report the end-of-acquisition minute (50), GSBP
  reports the start (40).
- *Observed:* the realtime `.spec` and `.data_spec` files for 41010 stamp the
  same records 10 minutes apart (xx:40 vs xx:50). **Products must not be joined
  on exact timestamps without checking.**

Project handling: wave observations carry `time_reference = "nominal"` and
`averaging_period_s = 1200`. Synchronisation keeps both timestamps and the
offset (`docs/methodology/synchronization.md`).

## Upstream file formats

Whitespace-delimited ASCII, gzipped for historical years. Header formats
changed over time, and every variant below is handled and tested on real
files:

| Era | Header |
|---|---|
| ≤ 1998 | `YY MM DD hh WD WSPD … BAR …` (2-digit year, no minute) |
| 1999–2004 | `YYYY MM DD hh WD …` |
| 2005–2006 | `YYYY MM DD hh mm WD …` |
| ≥ 2007 | `#YY MM DD hh mm WDIR …` plus a `#yr mo dy hr mn …` units row |

Spectral headers carry the frequency grid, which **also changed**. For example
41010 in 2003 used 0.03–0.40 Hz at 0.01 Hz; the later 47-band grid runs
0.02–0.485 Hz. Some station-years have a **second segment file with a `b`
before the year** (e.g. `41013jb2005.txt.gz`), which usually holds data after a
payload change on a different grid. The inventory found 118 such files (61 in
`swden`). They are indexed as period `2005b`, segment `b`, never merged
silently.

Plain HTTPS; no API key:

https://www.ndbc.noaa.gov/data/realtime2/{STATION}.{ext}
https://www.ndbc.noaa.gov/data/historical/{dir}/{station}{code}[b]{YYYY}.txt.gz

contain upper-case letters (e.g. `4cONFh2020.txt.gz`).

## Authentication

None.

## Quality control

NDBC applies automated and manual QC before release (Earle 2003 and NDBC QC
documentation). Values that fail QC are generally removed (replaced by missing
codes) rather than flagged, so **public files carry no per-value provider
flags**. The project applies QARTOD bulk-wave tests and physical checks on top
(`docs/QUALITY_CONTROL.md`, `configs/qc/wave_qc.yaml`).

## Missing data representation

- Realtime: `MM`.
- Historical: runs of 9s whose width depends on the variable (99.0, 999, 9999.0,
  …). They are matched **per column**. For example, `WDIR = 999` is missing
  but `WDIR = 99` is a valid direction.
- Spectral: 999 / 999.0 / 999.00.

## Known changes in instrumentation

Hull, payload, and frequency grids change between deployments. The inventory
records `segment` for mid-year grid changes, and parsers split a file into
several blocks when the grid changes. Deployment-level metadata (dates,
payloads) is not in the machine-readable files. Building a `Deployment` table
from NDBC station pages is future work (v0.2).

## Known limitations

1. **Historical r₁ and r₂ are scaled ×100** in monthly and yearly historical files
   (NDBC measdes). Realtime files are not scaled. Un-scaling is applied on
   parse and verified in tests.
2. **WVHT definition vs computation.** NDBC describes WVHT as the average of
   the highest one-third of waves. However, 4√m₀ of the published spectrum
   (midpoint bin widths) reproduces WVHT to reporting precision:

   | Station, year | n | mean(Hm0 − WVHT) | SD | max \|Δ\| |
   |---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
   | 41010 (Atlantic), 2023 | 4,281 | +0.0002 m | 0.0054 m | 0.053 m |
   | 46042 (Pacific), 2022 | 8,955 | +0.0009 m | 0.0084 m | 0.074 m |
   | 42036 (Gulf), 2022 | 10,166 | −0.0014 m | 0.0062 m | 0.080 m |
   | 44025 (Atlantic), 2021 | 8,542 | −0.0007 m | 0.0051 m | 0.062 m |

   Reproduce with `scripts/validate_ndbc_hm0.py <station> <year>`. WVHT is
   therefore tagged as a spectral estimator (Hm0). It is **not** comparable
   without adjustment to time-domain H₁/₃ from a displacement record, which
   is about 5 % lower (Goda 2010).
3. **No historical camera archive.** Only the latest image per station is
   listed, and superseded images disappear after about 72 h. See
   [Buoy-camera archiving](#buoy-camera-archiving).
4. **Burned-in caption.** Camera images carry station ID and UTC time
   rendered into the pixels. A model can read them. Mask the bottom band
   before training. It is exactly 30 rows on all 47 daylight 2880 × 300 images
   measured (`CAPTION_BAND_PX`). For other formats the band is scaled
   proportionally, which is unverified.
5. Provider QC removes data rather than flagging it, so difficult conditions
   may already be absent from the numeric record.
6. Directional estimates at low-energy frequencies are noisy.
   `qc.waves.directional_moment_test` flags coefficients that are not
   realisable.

## Buoy-camera archiving

Images are JPEG strips of **six camera views** facing different headings, named
`{CAM}_{YYYY}_{MM}_{DD}_{HHMM}.jpg`. They are usually stamped at minute 10 of the hour
(97 %; also :00, rarely :50, varying per camera from hour to hour). In the first archive run (2026-09-26 00:10 UTC, 79
images), 78 strips were 2880 × 300 px (6 × 480 px views) and one (44008) was
2304 × 246 px, so geometry is read per image. The views show independent
horizon tilt (buoy roll and pitch), sun glint, distant land, hull structure at
the edges, and occasional lens spots. `sources.ndbc.buoycam.split_views` crops
the caption and returns the six views, so QC and models can work per view.

A new image is posted every hour, day and night, about 20 minutes after its
time stamp. **Superseded images remain downloadable for about 72 hours** and
then return 404 (measured 2026-09-26). The directory cannot be listed, so the
archiver reconstructs earlier file names and backfills the last 70 hours on
every run. A collector outage shorter than about 3 days therefore loses nothing.

The collector runs hourly as a systemd user timer, and a second timer
snapshots the 45-day realtime spectral files for every camera station on the
1st and 15th of each month. Setup, monitoring, storage volumes, and the
offsite copy are covered in
[Continuous data collection](../operations/data_collection.md).
Pair images with spectra using those realtime snapshots, or with the annual
historical files once NDBC publishes them (preferred, because they are quality-controlled).

## Wave_Analysis transformations

| Stage | Transformation |
|---|---|
| parse | sentinels → NaN; 2-digit years → 19xx; `WD`→`WDIR`, `BAR`→`PRES`; r ÷ 100 (historical); compass points → degrees (`.spec`) |
| normalize | CF standard names; units → canonical (`mi`/`nmi` → m, `ft` → m, `degT` → degree mod 360); `method = provider_reported` |
| derive | Hm0, Tp (discrete and Young-weighted), Tm01, Tm02, Tm−10, ν, Qp, sₚ, Dm, Dp, spread; `method` records each estimator |
| QC | QARTOD Tests 9, 15, 16, 19, 20; steepness; spectral consistency; directional realisability |

## Validation tests

`tests/unit/test_ndbc_parsers.py` covers every format era on real, trimmed
files, the sentinel edge case (`WDIR = 99`), r scaling, grid changes, and
Hm0 = WVHT. `tests/unit/test_ndbc_inventory_source.py` covers index parsing,
the `b`-segment naming, normalisation schema validity, unit conversions,
QC wiring, and a mocked fetch. `tests/integration/test_ndbc_live.py`
(`pytest -m network`) detects upstream format changes.

## Suitable ML tasks

- H_s / T_p / direction regression from **buoy-camera** imagery against
  same-platform spectra, which avoids spatial separation entirely.
- Spectral targets S(f) and S(f, θ) (Level 5–6 tasks).
- Context covariates (wind, pressure) for stratified evaluation or multimodal inputs.
- Reference labels for nearby shore cameras, if propagation and depth
  differences are modelled.

## Unsuitable / risky uses

- Treating WVHT as time-domain H₁/₃.
- Joining products on exact timestamps.
- Random frame-level splits of camera images (hourly images of one station are
  strongly autocorrelated).
- Using a buoy's own wind as a model *input* when predicting its wave height
  without declaring it (this is multimodal regression, not vision-only).
- Training on unmasked camera captions.

## Open research questions

- How stable are camera geometry and field of view per station and deployment?
- How much of the image strip is usable (sea vs sky vs buoy structure) per camera?
- Can the rotation of a moving buoy be estimated from the horizon to
  compensate platform motion, as Yang et al. (2026) did with an IMU?
- Do NDBC wave processing changes between payloads introduce label
  discontinuities that a model would learn?
